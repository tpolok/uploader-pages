English

Uploader is a personal, self-hosted tool that lets its owner upload and schedule their own videos to their own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3.

It runs locally on the owner's computer. It is not a public service, has no user accounts, and is not offered to third parties.

It requests access to a YouTube account only to (1) upload videos to the channel and (2) read basic channel information to identify the connected channel.

See the Privacy Policy.
